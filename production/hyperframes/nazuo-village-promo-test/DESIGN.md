# Style Prompt

Warm handcrafted rural-game promo with a calm cinematic pulse: paper texture, courtyard sunlight, hand-painted village details, restrained UI overlays, and grounded human warmth. The video should feel like an invitation to step into a small living place rather than a loud game trailer.

## Colors

- Background ink: `#17201B`
- Paper mist: `#EFE5D0`
- Sunlit gold: `#D6A14C`
- Bamboo green: `#4F6F52`
- Clay red: `#9B4F37`

## Typography

- Headline: `League Gothic`, 800-900, tall and poster-like.
- Body and UI labels: `Montserrat`, 350-600, plain and readable.

## What NOT to Do

- No neon sci-fi palette or purple-blue gradients.
- No generic card grid layout.
- No pure black or pure white text.
- No overbusy particle field that hides the courtyard art.
