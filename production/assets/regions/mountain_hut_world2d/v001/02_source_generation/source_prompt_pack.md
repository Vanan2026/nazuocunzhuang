# MountainHut Source Prompt Pack v001

## Production Prompt

Paint a warm low-saturation fixed 3/4 top-down 2D storybook countryside scene on a full `1800x1200` opaque canvas. The scene is a quiet MountainHut just east of the Village. A soft dirt-and-stone path enters from the west edge, matching the Village east road in width, color, and detail density, then bends gently toward a small hut door. Include a modest hut exterior, readable door, small porch edge, herb shelf, quiet woodpile, soft grass, pebbles, flowers, and a little upper-right foreground bough for depth.

Use the layout lock and seam brief as spatial guides. Preserve the west seam road, hut door access, and clear standing pocket. Keep the painting natural and hand-made; do not trace every coordinate as hard geometry.

## Negative Prompt

No combat, monsters, weapons, armor, blood, damage, danger signs, high-pressure countdown, harsh survival cues, sci-fi HUD, readable quest text, cropped composition, isolated object stickers, or independent layer compositions.
